# GSR – Galileo Space Research
## Exploring the Universe | Understanding Space | Discovering Beyond Earth

##About GSR
• Galileo Space Research (GSR) is an independent space research and exploration initiative focused on understanding the universe, studying celestial objects, exploring scientific theories, and investigating mysteries beyond Earth.

• GSR aims to provide a structured platform for collecting, organizing, studying, and presenting scientific information related to space and astronomy.

• Our research covers everything from the Solar System to distant galaxies, black holes, extraterrestrial life, and theoretical concepts of space and time.

## Vision
To explore the unknown universe, expand scientific knowledge, and encourage independent research into space, astronomy, and advanced scientific theories.

## Mission
• Explore the mysteries of the universe.
• Study planets, stars, galaxies, and celestial objects.
• Research rockets, satellites, and space technology.
• Investigate the possibilities of extraterrestrial life.
• Study spacetime, wormholes, and theoretical physics.
• Collect and organize scientific research information.
• Promote scientific thinking and independent exploration.
